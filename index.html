Our Team

The people behind your smile.

Jamestown Family & Cosmetic Dentistry provides comprehensive dental services with a commitment to personalized, high-quality care. We focus on building lasting relationships while helping you achieve your perfect smile through expert general, cosmetic, and orthodontic treatment.

DDS · University of North Carolina, 1993

Dr. Michael Puckett

Dr. James M. Puckett is a dedicated and highly experienced dentist serving the Jamestown community from his practice on West Main Street. Since earning his degree from the University of North Carolina in 1993, he has been committed to comprehensive, patient-focused care with an emphasis on prevention and long-term oral health. Known for his compassionate approach, Dr. Puckett and his team prioritize comfort and individualized treatment to ensure every visit is a positive, trusted experience.

DDS · East Carolina University

Dr. Sidney Chadwick

“I'm Dr. Sidney Chadwick, and I am thrilled to welcome you to our practice. After earning my degree from East Carolina University and completing a residency at the VA Medical Center, I developed a deep passion for detail-oriented, patient-centered care. I love building lasting relationships with patients of all ages, and I am committed to treating your family with the same care and attention I give my own.”
